## 📚 Published Whitepapers

### 1. 🧠 [How We Reach AGI: Why Certification Is Step Zero](how-to-reach-agi.md)
* 📅 **Publication Date:** September 25, 2026
* ✍️ **Author:** Rifat Erdem Sahin ([@Erdem](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rifaterdemsahin/))
* 📖 **Interactive Web Reader:** [https://rifaterdemsahin.github.io/whitepaper/how-to-reach-agi.html](https://rifaterdemsahin.github.io/whitepaper/how-to-reach-agi.html)
* 📝 **Raw Markdown:** [how-to-reach-agi.md](how-to-reach-agi.md)
* 💡 **Synopsis:** A staged ten-step transition roadmap (Steps 0–9) demonstrating why frontier-lab certification is not a decorative credential, but the non-negotiable governance anchor and control mechanism required for trustworthy autonomous agents and scalable AGI deployment.

#### 🗺️ The 10-Step Roadmap At A Glance:
* 🎓 **Step 0: Certification** — Frontier-lab verified competence (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind)
* 👥 **Step 1: New Workforce** — Human-AI collaborative operating models
* 🏗️ **Step 2: Foundation** — Compute fabrics, vector stores, and data infrastructure
* 💻 **Step 3: CV as Code** — Public Git-versioned living engineering record
* 🧠 **Step 4: Second Brain** — Systematic knowledge management (PKM)
* 🔄 **Step 5: Reverse Training** — Re-engineering architectures from first principles
* 🤖 **Step 6: Agents** — Autonomous, multi-step agentic workflows
* 📊 **Step 7: RAGAS** — Groundedness and retrieval-augmented evaluation gates
* 🧩 **Step 8: AOM (Adaptive Object Model)** — Unified self-reconfiguring runtime engine
* 🌐 **Step 9: AGI** — General multi-domain problem-solving intelligence
